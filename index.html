How it works

We use MediaPipe to map 21 3D landmarks on your hands in real-time. Pinch your fingers to trigger a visual shockwave and a synth zap. Bring your hands closer together to modulate the continuous audio frequency. Everything is rendered via HTML5 Canvas.

Audio Engine

Powered by the Web Audio API. If you raise one hand, the vertical position acts like a Theremin. If you raise two hands, the distance between them controls the pitch and volume.
