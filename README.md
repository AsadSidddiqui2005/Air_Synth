# Air Synth

Play music with your hands. No keyboard, no mouse, just you and your camera.

## What's this?

Air Synth is a simple web app where you control sounds by moving your hands. Turn on your camera, give it permission, and start waving your hands around. Your movements become music.

## How it works

- Your webcam watches your hands
- Move them around to change the sounds
- That's it, really

## Getting started

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Let it access your camera
3. Wave your hands and make some noise

There's also an `intro.html` page if you want a quick walkthrough first.

## What you need

- Any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.)
- A webcam
- Internet connection

## That's all

No install, no setup. Just open the file and start playing.
